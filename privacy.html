RetroNest Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 November 2026

1. Who we are

RetroNest ("the app", "we") is an Android app for playing games you own with emulators. It is provided by Muhammed Emir Akdoğan (Türkiye). Contact: emirakd@proton.me.

2. The short version

  • Your games (ROM / disc files) stay on your device. The app never uploads them.
  • We run no servers that store your personal data. We have no accounts and no sign-up.
  • The app uses a few Google services, described below. They process some data under their own privacy policies.

3. What is collected, by whom, and why

ServiceDataPurpose
Google AdMob and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) – ads in the free version Advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, ad interactions, and your consent choice. Depending on your region and choices, ads may be personalized or not. Showing ads and measuring them; asking for and storing your consent where the law requires it. Premium removes ads. You can review or change your choices in Settings → Privacy choices.
Firebase Crashlytics – crash reports (only if "Send crash reports" in Settings is on) Crash stack traces, device model, Android version, app version, a random installation identifier, and the short name of the emulator core in use. Not included: game names, file names or file contents. Finding and fixing crashes. You can switch it off at any time in Settings.
Google Play Billing – Premium purchases Handled by Google Play. The app receives the purchase status and a purchase token; we do not receive your payment details. Unlocking Premium and keeping it active, restoring purchases.
Google Drive (appDataFolder) – optional save backup (Premium) Only if you connect it: a zip file with your game saves and cheats is stored in a hidden, app-private folder of your own Google Drive. The app asks for the narrow drive.appdata permission only; it cannot see your other Drive files. We cannot read the backup either. Backing up and restoring your saves on your request. Disconnect in Settings, and remove the permission in your Google Account at any time.
Cover art and announcements The app downloads box-art images (thumbnails.libretro.com) and a small announcements file (emira4i.github.io). Like any web request, these reveal your IP address to those hosts. No identifier of yours is sent. Showing cover art and news.

4. Data on your device

Your library, settings, save states, cheats, control layouts and imported game copies are stored only on your device. Android may include some app data in its own backup to your Google account (we exclude the Premium status cache, the Drive connection and the folder permissions from that backup).

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal data and do not share it with anyone except the Google services listed above, which act under their own terms and privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

6. Retention and your rights

We keep no personal data on our own servers. Data held by Google (ads, crash reports, purchases, Drive backup) is kept according to Google's policies; you can delete the Drive backup from your Google Account, reset your advertising ID in Android settings, and turn crash reports off in the app. Depending on where you live you may have rights of access, correction, deletion and objection; for data we control, contact us at emirakd@proton.me.

7. Children

RetroNest is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
